# Clear Thinking and Clear Expression

**If I cannot say it smoothly, I do not yet have the structure.**

This does not mean I am unintelligent. It means the idea has not yet been organised properly.

# Part 1: Clear Thinking

## The Goal of Clear Thinking \- passive skill(Structure) 

The goal is not to think harder. The goal is to think in layers.

When a topic/theme becomes complex, confusion often happens because different layers get mixed together:

* Facts(Objective to topic)(outside world):  
  * Definition \- unpack keywords of topic (understand)  
  * Example   
  * Exception \- idea is inapplicable/idea has counterargument   
- Subjective to topic(personal voice):  
* Opinion \- linked to subpoint, preferably backed with logical reasoning, supported with definition and example  
* Decision(conclusion, overall consensus)

Clear thinking means separating these layers before trying to solve the whole thing.

## 1\. The Basic Mental Template (Structure) ⭐

Use this silently in your mind:

**Theme/topic**  
**Your opinion/idea**  
**Reasoning to back up idea+ example**  
**counter-arguement(balance)**  
**Conclusion(overall consensus)**

This is the default thinking structure.

## 2\. The “One-Sentence First” Rule(Clarity)⭐

After applying a thinking template, Ask: **Can I say this in one clean sentence?**

## 3\. The Layer Separation Drill(Deeper thinking)

Ask: **What where who why when how to your topic/reasoning/example**

- Come up with questions using 5W1H yourself 

**Am I thinking about what, why, how, example, exception, or conclusion?**

## 4\. The Mental Stop Signal

Use this sentence when you notice muddling:

**I am mixing layers. Let us go back and review (Point 2: Basic mental template)**

# Part 2: Clear Expression

## 1\. The Goal of Clear Expression

Clear expression means the listener can follow your thinking without guessing your structure.

When speaking, do not begin with all details. Speak in the same order you think. Don’t rush yourself. (systematic)

**Theme/topic**  
**Your opinion/idea**  
**Reasoning to back up idea+ example**  
**counter-arguement(balance)**  
**Conclusion(overall consensus)**

This gives structure to you and to the listener.

# Part 3: Templates for Different Situations

## A. Explaining a Problem

Use this when something is broken, unclear, delayed, risky, or not working.

**Template:**

**Situation:** What is happening? **Expected:** What should have happened? **Actual:** What happened instead? **Impact:** Why does it matter? **Evidence:** What do we know so far? **Possible causes:** What may be causing it? **Next step:** What should we check or do next?

## B. Explaining an Existing Concept ⭐

Use this when teaching, reviewing, or clarifying an idea.

**Template:**

**Simple definition:** What is it? **Purpose:** Why does it exist? **Simple example:** Where do we see it? **How it works:** What are the main moving parts? **Common confusion:** What do people usually misunderstand? **Exception:** When does the simple explanation break?

# Part 4: Daily Practice Routine

## 5-Minute Clear Thinking Practice

Pick one topic daily. It can be from work, reading, news, family, health, investing, or any conversation.

Use the techniques for Part1 : Clear Thinking

## 5-Minute Clear Expression Practice

Take the same topic and explain it aloud.

Use Part1 Clear thinking for thinking it first and organise thoughts and then Part 2  Clear Expression 

Then ask:

**Did I say it smoothly?** **Where did I get stuck?** **Was the stuck point a definition, example, reason, exception, or conclusion problem?**

The stuck point reveals where the structure is weak.

## One-Sentence Clarity

Practice saying any idea(thought in the thinking structure) in one sentence.

Daily question: **Can I say the whole thing in one clean sentence?**

## Smooth Speaking Practice

Explain one topic aloud for two minutes daily.

Rule: **If I cannot say it smoothly, I do not yet have the structure.**

Do not blame memory or vocabulary first. First check the structure.

# Part 7: Personal Rules for Improvement⭐

Use the step by step method you’ve painstakingly come up with: 

1. First, use **Thinking structure**  
2. Then use **5W1H**  
3. Then **Condense thinking structure’s result into one sentence**   
4. If not smooth, return to the structure.  
5. Remember **PACC** while presenting ideas to the person  
6. During discussion, Remember **Self, Others, Context**  
7. Don’t rush to refute or explain yourself  
8. Lead the conversation to a consensus 

    Remember: Clear thinking is trained through repetition, not through one-time effort\!
